## What 1.3 is for

1.2 is on her phone (2026-10-20, PR #201). Her one complaint, in the user's words: "it takes too
long to get the once a month full moon bugs. Maybe make 2 weeks the maximum in between." The
rest of the week goes to polish she will see or feel: her phone's frame, the creatures and
scenery she has said look funny, the leafless trees A2 dealt too freely, and the personal
touches that have been parked since decision 177 (the user answers whichever they like in chat,
and a session puts them in). Refactors that change nothing she sees come last, if at all.

**Sub-agents fit to the task** (the user's ask): a rule, a cache, a doc or a mechanical refactor
is a **Sonnet** session; anything drawn, judged by eye or touching many files at once is an
**Opus** session. The model is named on each session below.

## The sessions

Each is one context window, a draft PR into `v1.3-dev` merged by the session when green, two at a
time. Decisions from **360**, one per session.

**F1. Visitors every fortnight (S, Sonnet).** `isVisitDay` (`src/systems/critters.ts`) is the
day after the full moon (R5, decision 310), so a critter out of its season or weather can be up
to 29 days away. It becomes the day after the full moon **and** the day after the new moon
(`moonAge` in `systems/calendar.ts` within half a day of 0, shifted a day), so nothing is more
than about fifteen days off. Moon-bound critters keep the full moon's own night. `nextChance`,
the Cabinet's "next on the 24th", Today's "critters come visiting" line and the small talk that
mentions the moon say the right day; the rarity test's simulated year is rerun (the Cabinet
should fill sooner than ten months; say by how much). A `1.2.1` `NOTES` row, three lines, the
mayor's voice: the moon's visitors twice a month. ⬆ **1.2.1**, the same day.

**F2. Her phone's frame (M, Sonnet).** "Where it hurts" 2 and 3: `happeningsAt(hour, day)` kept
for the hour and the day (a `Map` keyed `${day}:${hour}`, dropped when either turns), so a step
stops filtering every happening row for every neighbour; `Decorations` and `Mounds` keep the day
key by the minute as `Stalls` does, so `canWalk` stops working it out per call. `finale.test.ts`
drops its stated 30 s timeout if the cause was this. Proof: the suite, smoke, the lived-in
fixtures and W1's snapshots unchanged; `npm run perf` before and after (town update at Close,
21:30, is 1.1–1.4 ms at 1.2); the suite's wall time. Nothing she sees changes; no notes line.

**F3. The creatures (L, Opus).** She has said the creatures and scenery sometimes look funny.
Every critter (`src/sprites/critters.ts`, `crawlies.ts`, 75 rows at 16 and 24), every fish
shadow, the pets (`pets.ts`, each frame, each accessory) and the museum's cases looked at
together at 4× and 6× (`npm run sprite -- 'critter:*' 'pet:*' --zoom=6`, the gallery), ranked,
and the weakest dozen or so redrawn to `docs/art_style.md` (L6's method, decision 292: what
reads wrong at 32 on a phone: a silhouette that doesn't say what it is, a face lost in the body,
colours that fight the ground). The Cabinet, the museum, the bag icons and the world all show the
same art, so one redraw lands everywhere. Her look and every figure untouched (`looksHeld`).
Which were redrawn and why, in the decision; a line in 1.3's notes.

**F4. The scenery (S, Opus).** The art notes after 1.2: the broken and dead tree forms are dealt
to a ninth of every tree each, so a third of the town's eighteen trees are leafless; deal the
broken and leaning forms to the woods' edges only, and the dead one to the graveyard and the
woods (a rule in `lookOf`/`formOf`, not a redraw), so the town is leafy. Whisperwood's creek
ends square against the hedge at the east edge: a curl of bank. Then a look at every place
whole (`npm run sprite -- 'place:*'`, both orientations at Close) for anything else that reads
wrong and is small; fix what's small, log the rest. No notes line unless she'd notice.

**F5. Personal touches (M, Opus; when the user has answered).** The parked questions are in
`docs/handoff.md`, "Still to put to the user" (81–110). The user answers whichever they like in
chat; the coordinator records the answers in `docs/personal_touches.md` and this session puts
each in as a row (an outfit, a bake, a crop, a seat, a doll, a flower, a line), warm, gently
silly, spooky-cute, roughly one find in five hers (decision 15), real names and lyrics never (a
nod, a pun, a colour). A line in 1.3's notes, worded so the surprise keeps. If no answers come,
F5 doesn't run.

**F6. The forwarders and the small things (S, Sonnet; if time).** "Where it hurts" 4 and 7:
`Home`'s twenty-seven and `Neighbourhood`'s ten one-line forwarders dropped, their readers
pointed at `home.rooms` and `world.talk`; `keepTown` stamps the save with the world's clock so a
`?day=` town is saved as played then; 1.2's lived-in save added to the overlays snapshot. The
fixtures and snapshots are its proof. Nothing she sees changes.

**V1.3. Review and 1.3 (S, Opus).** Last: the 1.0 lived-in town opened as her phone will,
perf against 1.2's table, "Where it hurts" and the art notes rewritten, the `1.3` `NOTES` row
(the creatures, the touches, the trees), the handoff for whoever comes after (there may be no
one for a while: write it for a session a year from now), `CLAUDE.md`, decision for the release,
the release PR. ⬆ **1.3**.

## Running it

As 1.1 was run (`docs/handoff.md`, "How 1.1 was run"): the coordinating session starts each
session as a sub-agent in a worktree of its own, two at a time, the model named above; the rules
are `docs/v1.1_plan.md`'s "Running it in parallel" with `v1.3-dev` for `v1.1-dev`. The save stays
v55 unless a touch needs a field (then F5 bumps it, last). `main` gets 1.2.1 and 1.3 at the
user's word.
