## What 0.3 is for

She's still playing, and two things have her: **collecting the critters**, and **buying new
things and decorating her house**. 0.3 leans into both, fixes the eight things the two of them
hit, and adds what fits round the edges: a proper farm, and a neighbour who comes with the
release (decision 211).

Three things shape how it's built:

- **Sessions are small.** Every session below fits one context window and ends with a green PR
  into `v0.3-dev`. A session can be started and stopped at any time; nothing waits on a session
  that isn't finished (`CLAUDE.md`, "Checkpoint as you go").
- **Lanes run side by side.** The sessions are grouped into five lanes by the files they own, so
  several Opus 5.5 sessions can run at once the way 0.2's three did (decision 163,
  `docs/parallel_plan.md`). The lanes and their rules are at the end of this plan.
- **The code stays clean.** Every session runs the design review checklist, the wiring split
  decision 210 asked for is done first so five lanes aren't all adding to one 380-line
  constructor, and content lands as rows in a const of its own so lanes merge by adding lines.

## The workflow for 0.3

- **`v0.3-dev` is the integration branch** (made from `main` on 2026-10-04, after 0.2.5). Each
  session branches from it, its PR targets it, and it's merged with a merge commit as soon as it's
  green. `main` (her phone) gets a **release** only when the user says so, as one PR from
  `v0.3-dev`, because Vercel deployments are limited. ⬆ marks a good moment to release; the user
  picks. Each release adds its own `NOTES` row in `src/data/patchNotes.ts` (0.3, then 0.3.x).
- **CI runs on every PR**, drafts included (decision 139). The container runs the whole suite,
  smoke included, before every push.
- **Vercel previews stay off** for `claude/**` and the dev branches (`vercel.json`).
- **Personal touches stay parked** (decision 177): no session asks for one, and none adds a
  question. Where a touch would go, the session picks the warmest sensible default and names it
  in its decision.
- **Saves:** 0.3 begins at save v35. Several lanes change the save's shape, so the rule from 0.2
  (one lane bumps) becomes: a session that changes the save **bumps in its last commit, after
  merging the latest `v0.3-dev`**, taking the next number, and the coordinator merges
  save-bumping PRs **one at a time**. The release session adds a lived-in fixture (decision 210).

## Principles for every session

The 0.1 and 0.2 principles hold (`docs/v0.1_plan.md`, `docs/v0.2_plan.md`): rules in `world/`
and `systems/`, never in drawing code; time from the injected clock and the day key; content as
rows; saves versioned and migrated with a test; callers go to the service; a callout is fixed at
its cause; content that grows gets a shape first; the player can always reach the edge; and
**nothing punishes, expires or is lost** (decision 11). For 0.3, three more:

- **Nothing is gated** (decision 211). A new place is open from the first day; a new shop sells
  from the first day; what grows with play is what she collects and what she makes, never what
  she may do. A recipe made from materials (an extension, a room) is making, not gating.
- **Every collection has somewhere to be seen.** A thing she collects (a critter, a squishy, a
  doll, a record, a fossil) can be put on show at home, and a set has a shelf that fills as she
  finds them.
- **Rows live in a const of their own.** A session's furniture, items, critters or recipes go in
  a named const in their data file (as `GIFTED`, `KEEPSAKES` and `MILESTONE_PIECES` already do),
  spread into the table, with their art in a file of their own. Two lanes adding to the same
  table then merge by adding lines.

### The design review checklist (in every session's PR)

The 0.1 checklist, unchanged: wrong layer? a file with a second job? new state saved, migrated,
tested, repaired? deterministic from the clock and day key? content as data, art in `sprites/`,
drawn by a test? the next three features? frame time or memory? what got harder?

## The big forks, settled

Asked with the draft on 2026-10-04 and answered the same day; each is a decision (212–217). The
recommendation in each stood.

1. **The farm is a new place, and Hosta La Vista Farm stays by her house.** Her beds are spread
   over the farm (16, plus two extension rows), the creek (4) and the lake (4), and tending them
   is a walk. The fix is a place made for it: **a farm of its own, west of town down the main
   road** (the town's west edge at rows 14–15 is free, mirroring the way to Whisperwood), with
   rows and rows of beds, a greenhouse to go into, an orchard, a pond, a barn and a seed cart.
   The farm by her house keeps its beds and everything in them (nothing is lost) as her kitchen
   garden, and the plots by the creek and lake stay too (the lake's moves a few tiles, decision 217).
   _Alternative:_ move every bed to the new place and give the fenced farm beside her house to
   her yard (fork 3). Recommended: keep them; a bed she planted yesterday should be where she
   left it. _Also to pick:_ the place's name. The default is **Boo Acres**.
2. **The neighbour who comes with 0.3 is the farmer.** Decision 211 says a new neighbour comes
   with a release, themed to it. The theme is the farm, so the default is **Scarah, a scarecrow
   who came to life one harvest moon** (burlap, a straw bob, a patched sundress, a crow called
   Cornelius on her shoulder who says one word), who lives in the farmhouse at Boo Acres, loves
   beetles and sweetcorn, sells seeds from her cart, and has a place in every happening from the
   moment the release lands. _Alternative:_ a critter-keeper (a moth-winged lepidopterist) at the
   farm instead, or no neighbour this release. Recommended: Scarah.
3. **Decorating grows in three directions: on tables, into a second room, and out into her
   yard.** All three are in the plan (H3, H4, H5), in that order, because each is a shape the
   next builds on. If the user wants only some, the lane stops after the ones they want.
4. **Fossils are a new collection.** Digging already exists (`world.digging`, the buried keys);
   0.3 makes it daily: a few mounds a day round the places she has found, each with a fossil for
   Wrapunzel's museum (a seventh case) or a bead. Recommended: yes, it's the collecting she loves
   with no new verb to learn.
5. **The lake's plot moves, and the test learns to see the break.** The four beds at Lantern
   Shore's south-west corner and the lamp beside them cut the west bank off from the rest of the
   shore (only the frozen creek joins them). The beds move up the west bank, the lamp a tile
   over, and `tests/data/zones.test.ts` flood-fills without ice and with the lots' houses up, so
   it can't happen again. Beds planted at the old tiles are moved with them (a migration).
6. **How many lanes at once.** Five lanes are drawn below; 0.2 ran three (decision 163) because
   the usage limit goes as fast as there are lanes. The chains are independent, so any number
   from one to five can run; the coordinator starts the next lane when one finishes.

## The sessions

Each is one PR into `v0.3-dev`, one context window, with the checklist. **S** is a short session,
**M** a full one. ⬆ marks a good moment to release to `main`, if the user wants one. The letter
is the lane (W is before the lanes, V after).

### W. Before the lanes

**W1. The wiring split by area (S).** What decision 210 wrote down and left for "the next session
that adds a service": `build.ts`'s constructor becomes a function per area (the home's, the
passive Candy's, the calendar's and festival's, the fairground's) taking the shared parts and
returning its services, assigned in the constructor, those needing no forward reads first. Then

passes unchanged._ No lane starts before W1 merges.

### A. Her, and the view (lane 1: no save changes)

**A1. Boots under skirts (M).** `WORN_ORDER` draws shoes after every top, bottom and outer piece,
so a boot's shaft paints over any hem: knee-highs over a sundress, every shoe over the gown's
last row, even sneakers over a hem in a walk frame. The fix is at its cause: a shoe's shaft (the
leg rows it paints) is drawn **under** any skirted piece (the sundresses, the skater, pleated and
tulle skirts, the gown, the velvet dress, the wing dress, the opera coat, the cape from behind)
and **over** trousers, which paint every leg row; the foot and sole stay on top. A rule in
`layerOf` that reads what else is worn, not a special case per boot, with the `pieceRows` cache
key extended if a piece's rows come to depend on it. `tests/sprites/doll.test.ts` holds it with
`pixel` checks (the sundress with knee-highs matches the sundress with flats at the hem; over
jeans they differ). The gallery (`?gallery`) shows every skirt with every shoe before the PR is
marked ready.

**A2. Capes, wings and hair (M).** Outer pieces have one layer and no back: a cape fakes "behind
her" by clearing its own pixels where the body is, and sits under the hair, so from the front the
collar vanishes under a bob and from behind long hair covers the cape to the shoulders; gloves and
bracelets float over the cape from behind; the cape paints over a flared hem. Give capes and wings
a **back layer** drawn before the skin (so the body, skirts and hair cover it naturally) and a
**front layer** for the collar and lining drawn after the hair, with a collar wide enough to show
past every style (Cody's cape in `villagers.ts` is the pattern: `under`, hair, `over`); from
behind, the back layer comes after gloves, shoes and bracelets. The butterfly-wing dress gets the
same treatment from behind. Tests as A1's. After A1 (both in `doll.ts`).

**A3. See-through trees (S).** A tree's crown covers the one to three rows above its base, where
critters are dealt (`beside(props('tree'))`), where she walks, and where two dozen toadstools and
rocks stand in Whisperwood. In `OutdoorView.draw`, after the sort, an occluder pass: a tree, old
tree, willow or candy tree whose crown overlaps a drawable with a smaller `footY` that she might
want (her, a critter, a ready giver or patch, the bone, the snack, an egg, a neighbour) is drawn
at about half alpha, eased in and out over a few frames (view state, like the camera). Copies,
never mutation (`this.props` is shared every frame), a pure helper in `render/occlusion.ts` with
a test, Wes exempt (he's meant to be half hidden), and `drawLight`'s erase pass made to honour
`alpha` so a glowing moth behind a faded tree keeps its glow. _Done when smoke's screenshot shows
her through a crown._

**A4. Food that says what it does (S).** A dish's effect is a fact of its row, so every card for
a dish, snack or treat gets a fixed line from `effectOf` under its description ("Eat it: a spring
in your step till the window turns" / "the fish bite sooner" / "a moth comes out to see what
smells so good"), the stove sheet's groups say the same, and **a chip in the top bar** for each
buff that's on (its dish's icon and "till evening"), gone when the window turns, a tap on it
saying what it does. The `ate` moment goes through `moments.ts` like every other. _Done when
smoke eats a snack and finds the chip._ ⬆ (the fixes are on her phone)

## What this plan does not do

A second new neighbour, more rooms than two, decorating beyond her own yard, pets beyond the

### Rules every lane session follows

These go into each session's starting prompt, and the coordinating session writes them under "In
progress" in `docs/handoff.md` before the first lane starts.

1. Branch from the latest `v0.3-dev` (`git fetch origin v0.3-dev && git checkout -b
claude/<session> origin/v0.3-dev`); the PR targets `v0.3-dev`, is opened as a draft at the first
   push, and is merged by the session itself with a merge commit once green. Nothing goes to
   `main`.
2. Before starting: read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/handoff.md`'s "In progress" (its lane's heading and
   these rules), the session's paragraph here, and the decisions its forks settled (212–217).
   Personal touches are parked (decision 177): ask no questions, add none; pick the warmest
   default and name it in the decision.
3. The whole suite in the container before every push: lint, format:check, typecheck, test,
   build, and smoke with `CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`. CI runs on the draft too.
4. Commit and push at least every half hour, and update the lane's heading under "In progress"
   with each push (what's done, what's half done and where, the next steps).
5. Decisions in the lane's block, appended, never edited. Each session adds its line to the 0.3
   `NOTES` row; V1 folds them to five.
6. **A session that changes the save bumps in its last commit**, after merging the latest
   `v0.3-dev`, taking the next number, and says so in its handoff heading; the coordinator merges
   save-bumping PRs one at a time. Lane 1 never touches the save.
7. New rows go in a const of their own, new art in a file of its own, a new rule in a system of
   its own; a shared file gets lines added, never reshaped.
8. Merge `v0.3-dev` into the branch before marking the PR ready, resolve any conflict in the
   shared files, rerun the suite, then merge.
9. When the session's PR has merged: update this plan's status line, clear its heading under "In
   progress" to "<session> landed (PR #n). Next in this lane: <session>", and stop.

### The coordinating session

One session (a fresh one, started by the user) does what decision 163's did:

- Runs W1 itself, or starts it, and waits for it to merge.
- Writes the lane headings and rules into `docs/handoff.md` on `v0.3-dev` (one small PR).
- Starts each lane's first session with `create_session` (model `claude-opus-5-5`, the
  repository at `v0.3-dev`, `permission_mode` the same as its own, never `plan`), with a prompt
  naming the session, the lane, its decision block, and the rules above. Two lanes at once (decision 212):
  lanes 1 and 2 first, lane 3 when lane 1 finishes, lane 5 when lane 2 finishes, lane 4 last.
- Checks in about every half hour (`send_later`): is the current PR green and merged? If merged,
  start the lane's next session. If a session has gone quiet for over an hour with its handoff
  heading unchanged, read its PR and start a fresh session on the same branch to resume it.
- Merges save-bumping PRs one at a time, in the order they come ready.
- Starts V1 when every lane has finished.
- Never merges to `main`; the release is the user's.

### Risks, and what's done about each

- **Two sessions bumping the save to the same number.** Rule 6: the bump is the last commit after
  merging `v0.3-dev`, and the coordinator merges those PRs one at a time.
- **Five lanes adding to `furniture.ts`, `items.ts` and `build.ts`.** W1 first; a const and a
  file per session (rule 7); add-only in the shared files.
- **Lane 2 and lane 5 both drawing furniture.** Lane 2 draws display and outdoor pieces, lane 5
  the sets; each in its own sprite file, gathered by `sprites/furniture.ts`.
- **The museum re-laid twice (C1, C2).** Same lane, in order; C1 lays it out for eight cases and
  C2 fills the eighth.
- **A session cut off mid-way.** The half-hourly push and handoff heading; the coordinator
  resumes it on the same branch.
- **Usage.** Each lane draws as fast as a session; the user chose two at a time (decision 212)
  and can stop any lane at its next merge without leaving anything half-built.
