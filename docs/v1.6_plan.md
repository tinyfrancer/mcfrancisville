## The sessions

Every session is **Opus**, one context window, a draft PR into `v1.6-dev` merged when green, two
at a time by files. Decisions from **380**. The save is v58; a session that needs a field bumps it
in its last commit, one at a time (X3 first, v59).

**X1. The festival's lights, and the 31st rehearsed (M).** The Halloween Festival's coloured
lights (`eaveLights` in `src/sprites/holidays.ts`, strung under every building's eaves from its
roof keys and drawn by `eaveDrawables` in `src/render/holidays.ts`; the fairground's poles'
strings in `src/sprites/fairground.ts`) don't join up: find why by eye (`?day=2026-10-28&hour=20`,
every building in town and at the fair, at Close) and fix it so a string runs unbroken along each
eave and round its corners, meets the next pole, and never floats. Then **rehearse 31 October**
under `?day=2026-10-31` by taps: the costume contest, the party at the stage, Cody's half of her
costume on every build, the photo (`PhotoCard`) now that the camera exists (both must work; the
party photo keeps its own path), trick or treat at every door, the finale's set, the 1 November
letter, in every build and with Pickle about. Fix what's small; log the rest. A line in 1.6's
notes.

**X2. A ride on the big wheel (M).** The fairground's big wheel turns (E5) but she can't ride it:
a fifth activity (`ACTIVITIES` row on the wheel's prop, a few Candy, the day's first go free as
the stalls', R6b): she climbs into a car, the camera follows her up and round one slow turn (a
drawn scene in the sheet's stage as the fair games are, or in the world with the camera lifted:
pick what reads better at Close), the fair and the town laid out small below, lights at night;
Cody or a neighbour who's at the fair rides along and says something at the top; a keepsake the
first time. A line in 1.6's notes.

**X3. Chickens at Boo Acres (L; save v59).** A coop by Scarah's barn (a prop, `coop`, in
`BOO_ACRES`; art `src/sprites/farm.ts`), three hens with names and a rooster who crows at six
(`src/data/hens.ts`, drawn as the pets are, pecking about the yard by day and in by night), fed
once a day from her bag (any crop or seed) for an egg each the next morning (`world.henhouse`,
`Takings`), eggs an item that bakes (two recipes at the stove and one with Wrapunzel use eggs:
an egg custard tart, a frittata, meringue ghosts), Scarah's lines about them, Today's line when
eggs are waiting. Nothing dies or goes hungry (decision 11): an unfed hen just lays nothing. A
line in 1.6's notes.

**X4. Snow days, puddles and a rainbow (M).** In winter: a **snowman** she builds in her yard or
anywhere in town on a snowy tile (a tap on lying snow with empty hands rolls one in three taps;
a prop that stands till the thaw, then is swept into her chest as a snowman figurine keepsake,
nothing lost), neighbours at their stops throw snowballs at each other now and then (an
effect, a laugh), her footprints in snow (a decal that fades). After rain: she splashes in
puddles she walks through (`puddles.ts`, an effect and a sound), and a **rainbow** over the
place for the first ten minutes after rain stops on a day with sun (`src/render/sky.ts`-style
pass). A line in 1.6's notes.

**X5. Small delights (M).** Things that cost nothing and make her smile: a butterfly or moth
lands on her hat when she stands still a while outdoors in its season (and flies off as she
moves); fireflies gather round her on summer nights; Fibi brings her a "gift" (a leaf, a sock)
once a day at home; the crows on the scarecrow caw when she walks past; her pets greet her at
the door when she comes home; a sneeze in the long grass in spring (a pose and a "bless you"
from a neighbour in earshot). Each an effect or a short rule, no save. A line in 1.6's notes.

**X6. The museum's skeleton, and a kite (M).** With all twelve fossils donated, Wrapunzel
