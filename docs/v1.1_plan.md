## What 1.1 is for

1.0 finished the V1 plan and 1.0.1 fixed the callouts that were bugs. What's left logged is of
three kinds, and the user asked for all of it at once ("review/add everything besides the
leftover cloud save steps, including the where it hurts"), with two sound complaints from her
phone folded in:

1. **Two things she hears and shouldn't.** The crickets: three cricket songs loop together at
   about four chirps a second from eight at night until four in the morning, April to October,
   in every place outdoors (`CRICKET_SONGS` in `src/audio/ambience.ts`, 240 bpm, twelve chirps
   in six seconds each), and she plays mostly at night, so what was meant as a bed is a
   near-constant chirp, "almost non-stop and annoying". And **the silent switch doesn't silence
   the game**: V1's S1 went round it on purpose (decision 321, `audioSession` `playback` or a
   looping silent element), and she uses the switch to turn sound off. 1.1 respects the switch
   and tames the crickets first, as a patch of its own.
2. **The refactors in "Where it hurts"** (`docs/architecture.md`): the suite and smoke slow and
   smoke one file (2), one body under every look (3), arriving a chain of `if`s (5), eight
   overlays asked where she can walk (6), the planter's bed key without the room (10), `Home`
   holding four features (11), the big view, wiring and service files (12), the areas' order
   (13), the big data files and the one chunk (14), the legend's characters (15), pets walking
   tile to tile (17). Nothing she sees changes; everything after gets cheaper.
3. **The art notes that are a redesign** (`docs/handoff.md`, "Art notes for the final pass"): the
   lavender cobble carrying the town and the empty square, the woods' walls of one crown, the
   flat ice and creeks, the big empty lawns, the castle garden's four same beds, and the little
   pumpkin at the noticeboard's foot still orange in winter.

Left out on purpose: the cloud save's store and her phone's first `?perf` readings ("Where it
hurts" 1), which are the user's steps and stay in the handoff's "After 1.0".

Three things shape how it's built, as before: **sessions are small** (one context window, a
green PR into `v1.1-dev`, stoppable at any moment); **lanes run side by side, two at a time**
(decision 266), by the files they own; **patches, not a launch** (⬆ where a patch makes sense;
1.1 is the sound patch, 1.2 the rest).

## The workflow for 1.1

As V1's (`docs/v1_plan.md`, "The workflow for V1", and `CLAUDE.md`): `v1.1-dev` is the
integration branch, in `vercel.json`'s no-preview list; each session branches from it, PRs into
it as a draft at the first push, merges itself with a merge commit when green; `main` gets a
patch only at the user's word, as one PR from `v1.1-dev`, with a `NOTES` row per patch (three to
five lines, the mayor's voice; a refactor gets no line). Save bumps only in a PR's last commit,
one PR at a time. Decisions appended in the lane's block, never edited.

## Principles for every session

V1's hold (`docs/v1_plan.md`, "Principles for every session"). For 1.1, three more:

- **A refactor changes nothing she sees.** A session in lane W or T ships with the lived-in
  fixtures (`tests/persistence/livedIn.test.ts`) and smoke's screenshots unchanged but for what
  it says it changes; the PR says what it compared.
- **Measured before and after.** A refactor of a hot path (the view, `canWalk`, the suite) gives
  the number before and the number after, in its PR and in `docs/architecture.md`'s tables.
- **Heard before it merges.** A sound session renders what it changed to a WAV with an
  `OfflineAudioContext` under Playwright (or a Node WAV of the schedule), looks at the chirp
  density or the level over a minute, and says the numbers in its PR; smoke can't hear.

The design review checklist is unchanged (`docs/v1_plan.md`).

## The sessions

Lanes: **H** (heard: the sound), **T** (tooling: the suite and smoke), **W** (the world's shape),
**A** (the art). Each session is one context window.

### H. Heard (lane H; never the save)

**H1. The silent switch, Play on silent, and an ambience switch (S).** The game goes quiet
when the switch on the side of her phone is down: `SilentSwitch` (`src/audio/session.ts`) no
longer sets the session to `playback` or plays the silent element unless **Play on silent** is
on, so by default Web Audio stays in the "ambient" category the switch mutes (reversing decision
321's default at the user's word: she turns sound off with the switch; decisions 328 and 329).
Settings' Sound tab: **Play on silent** (off by default, per phone, `settings.ts`; on, S1's
bypass as built), the silent hint saying which way it's set, and a third switch beside Sounds
and Music, **Ambience** (on by default), muting the ambience bus (`graph.ts`'s `Mixer`) only.
`smoke:offline` and the sound tests brought in line. ⬆ with H2 (**1.1**).

**H2. The crickets tamed, and every bed listened to (M).** Crickets as they are: a chorus that
swells and rests, not a loop. One cricket near and one far (the third dropped), each a run of
four to eight chirps then a rest of twenty to sixty seconds, dealt from a slow clock so the two
rarely overlap; the chirp itself softer (`gain` 0.45 → about 0.25, the brush's `q` wider) and
lower in the bed (`CRICKETS_IN` halved; the town 0.3). Across all places, **no more than one
chirp a second averaged over a minute, and a rest of at least twenty seconds in every two
minutes**, held by a test on the schedule (`tests/audio/ambience.test.ts`). Then every other
layer looked at for the same fault, constancy: the fairground's murmur (0.55 from ten to
eleven), the wind on the castle hill (0.7), the lake's water, the hum indoors; each given a slow
swell and rest where it has none, levels trimmed, numbers in the PR. If she still finds a place
loud after 1.1, the Ambience switch (H1) is hers. ⬆ (**1.1**, with H1).

### T. Tooling (lane T; never the save)

**T1. Smoke split by area (M).** `scripts/smoke.mjs` (7,800 lines, 622 checks, twenty minutes)
becomes `scripts/smoke/` with the runner and its helpers (`boot`, `tapProp`, the viewports, the
screenshot and shimmer checks) in one file and a file per area (`boot`, `town`, `home`, `farm`,
`neighbours`, `critters`, `fair`, `seasons`, `sound`, `cloud`…), each a list of sections as
now. `npm run smoke` runs all; `npm run smoke -- town fair` runs some; CI runs all. No check
removed; the count the same before and after, in the PR. The gallery check pages the catalogue
(`?gallery&page=n`) instead of falling back to its top ("Where it hurts" 2).

**T2. The suite made quick (M).** 2,568 tests take about 340 s in the container. The slow files
found by `vitest --reporter=verbose` timings: the simulated years (`rarity`, `weeks`,
`rareEvents`) run once per file over a shared world or a cached deal; files that build a world
per case through `harness()` share one where cases don't touch each other; `livedIn` opens
each fixture once. Target: the suite under half what it is, no test removed, the count in the
PR. `docs/architecture.md`'s "Where it hurts" 2 rewritten with the numbers.

### W. The world's shape (lane W; W2 changes the save)

**W1. Arrivals as a table, overlays as a list, pets on a pulled path (M).** `World.arriveOn`'s
chain of `if`s (the pots, the candy tree, a sapling, the pumpkin patch, the bandstand, the stall,
a goose, a mound, a way out, gathering, the stray) becomes a table keyed by prop id, as phase K
made `arrivals` by visit kind, the mound's two owners asked in one handler, the order kept and
held by a test ("Where it hurts" 5). `MapZone.canWalk` and `propAt`'s eight overlays (the stalls,
a shut gate, the lots, the decorations, the farm's rows, her yard, the mound, the sites) become
one list of `Overlay`s, each with its own `propAt` and `blocks`, in the order they are now (6).
Pets walk a path pulled taut as she does (`stringPull`, 17). Nothing she sees changes; smoke's
screenshots say so.

**W2. Rooms out of `Home`, and a planter that knows its room (M; save v55).** `Home` (480 lines:
her rooms, the chest's two lists, what's on show, what stands on what) gives its rooms to a
`Room` keeper, `Home` keeping the chest and the shared things, `Yard` reusing the surface rules
through `Decorable` as now (11). The planter's bed key carries the room (`home:<room>:<tile>`),
so a planter may stand in the back room; `refusesHere` lifted; a migration step rekeys the front
room's beds (10). The save bump in the last commit, after merging `v1.1-dev`; the lived-in
fixtures still pass and a 1.0.1 fixture added if the shape changes beyond the key.

**W3. `Talk` out of `Neighbourhood`, `apis.ts` by area, the areas' order explicit (M).**
`services/Neighbourhood.ts` (1,100 lines) keeps who's where and the walking; the hello, the
heart moments, the questions, the replies, the calls and the talk actions move to a `Talk`
service in the same area (12). `wiring/apis.ts` (760 lines) becomes `wiring/apis/<area>.ts`
with one `buildApis` composing them, as `world/areas/` is. The areas' order (13), which decides
who hears a signal first, is written down once: each area function declares what it listens
for and a test holds that no area listens for a signal an earlier area emits before it (or the
order is named in `build.ts` with the why). `main.ts` (540 lines) loses what belongs in wiring.
The legend's dozen free characters (15) are noted in `maps.ts` as the rule for the next place
(its own legend), nothing more.

### A. The art (lane A; never the save)

**A1. The square and the cobble (M).** The town's roads, the square and the fairground's paths
are all one lavender cobble, and the square is a plaza with a small well in it. A second paving
for the square (flagstones, a ring of setts round the well), a worn middle on the roads
(`tracks.ts`'s thinning, as L2 did for dirt), benches or planters on the square from the clutter
props, and the square made a tile or two smaller if that reads better in `npm run sprite --
'place:town'`. The town's commonest colour measured before and after (L2's method). Both
orientations looked at.

**A2. The woods' edges, the lawns, the castle beds and the winter pumpkin (M).** Whisperwood,
the shore and the castle hill are edged with the same round teal crown; `treeForms.ts` gets two
or three more edge forms (a tall thin crown, a leaning one, a broken one) and the maps' edges an
uneven line, trees stepping in and out, with `tests/data/maps.test.ts` still holding the edge
solid. The hidden clearing's middle and the fairground's south-east get a meadow patch or a few
standing props; the castle garden's four beds become four shapes; the little uncarved pumpkin at
the noticeboard's foot joins `RESHAPED` for winter (1.0.1's X2 left it orange).

**A3. Ice and the creeks (S).** The frozen creek in Whisperwood and the creek feeding the lake
are a flat pale ribbon with hard edges beside water that has banks and glints (L6). Ice gets a
sheen and a few cracks (a `frames` glow, slow, as the water's glints), the creeks the banks' soft
edge, and the creek running into the open lake a melt at its mouth.

**A4. `OutdoorView` split (M).** `render/OutdoorView.ts` (1,240 lines) hands its passes to files
of their own as the lanes did for effects and the fair games: the yard, the mounds, the sites,
the see-through crowns, the fishing line and reel, film night, the fountain, the frames, each a
`Pass` with `draw(ctx, frame)` and what it sorts, `OutdoorView` composing them in order (12).
Nothing she sees changes; smoke's screenshots and `?perf`'s numbers the same before and after,
in the PR.

**A5a. A second body (L).** "One body under every look" (3): every cut, action pose, working
pose and face is painted onto one 32×48 body, so every neighbour below the neck is her
silhouette. A `Build` on a figure row (`'slight' | 'stout'` beside the default) picks a body
grid in `sprites/doll.ts`, each body region (upper arm, elbow, forearm…) redrawn for it so
every cut, `tailor`, `groom`, the action and working poses and `seated` paint onto it unchanged;
the size stays 32×48 (decision 79). This session does the stout build and gives it to two
neighbours (Gourdon and Barty, say), `doll.ts` split by family on the way (14: the body, the
cuts, the hair, the poses). Every look rendered before and after in `npm run sprite -- 'doll:*'`
and the villagers' sheet, looked at.

**A5b. The third body, and the town cast (L).** The slight build, and every neighbour and Cody
given the build that fits them (a row field, the default for her), the costumes (`COSTUMES`) and
Cody's halves checked on each, the busts (`drawBust`) following. The chunk (14) is noted, not
split: one 1.8 MB chunk precached once a version is the right shape for an installed app.

## What 1.1 is not

No new content: no neighbour, place, critter or furniture (a new neighbour comes with a release
that has a theme, decision 270; 1.1 has none). No gameplay change. No personal touches
(decision 177). The cloud save's store and the phone's first timings are the user's.
