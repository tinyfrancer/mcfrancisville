## The sessions

Two **Opus** sessions in parallel, one context window each, a draft PR into `v1.7-dev` merged
when green; then the release. Decisions from **388**. The save is v59; Y1 bumps it to v60 in its
last commit if it keeps the snowball and the rainbow.

**Y1. The rough edges she'd meet, and the last card (M).** The leftovers 1.6's review logged
(`docs/architecture.md` "Where it hurts", the art notes in `docs/handoff.md`), fixed where small:
a half-rolled snowball and the rainbow's ten minutes survive a reload (a field each, save v60);
company on the big wheel comes along more often (Cody whenever it's evening and he's not
elsewhere, else the closest neighbour anywhere at the fair, else she rides alone and the car opposite
holds a crow who says nothing at the top); the ride sheet's "on the house" line goes once the free ride
is spent; the party photo and the camera leave out the chatter bubbles; a kite or a seed in her
hand puts the rod in it before the cast is drawn (or the cast waits for empty hands with a
toast: pick what reads better). Then **the `1.7` `NOTES` row**, the last card for a while, four
lines at most in the mayor's voice plus Cody's own line: the mayor says this is his last note for
a while, the town keeps on without him (every day still turns, nothing stops), and he hands the
page to Cody, whose line is, exactly as he wrote it, in quotes and his: **"you are my everything,
and I want nothing more than to see you smile everyday."** Warm, not sad. The patch-notes test
holds the newest row and that a phone that saw 1.6 reads 1.7.

**Y2. The wishing well, and leaf piles (M).** Two warm things she'll see tonight, in October.
The **wishing well**: the square's well (`well` in `TOWN`, art `src/sprites/townProps.ts`) is
walked up to as a prop (a `PropArrivals` row in the town's area): once a day she may toss in a
Candy (a card, `src/hud/WellCard.ts` through a `WellApi`, Toss / Not today) and a wish comes back
as a line (`src/data/wishes.ts`, forty or so, gently silly, a few by season, holiday or weather,
dealt from the day key, never the same two days running), a coin's plink and a ripple of light
(`Effects`); about one toss in seven something comes back up (a pretty pebble, a lost bracelet
bead, once ever a `wellPenny` keepsake), nothing ever lost but the Candy. **Leaf piles**: in
autumn (`leavesFall`, `src/render/leaves.ts`) a pile gathers under a few of each place's trees
(an `Overlay` dealt from the day key, art in `src/sprites/nature.ts` or a new `leafPiles.ts`,
walkable); a tap on one walks her into it and she jumps (the `crouch` then a hop, leaves
scattering as `Effects` particles, a rustle cue), and once a day a pile has a critter or a
crawly in it that pops out for her to catch, or a thing someone dropped (a small event's lost
thing fits, `world.smallEvents`), never nothing the first time. Kept by `Takings` per window;
nothing new in the save. Shared add-only files as before; a line each in 1.7's card is NOT
wanted (Y1 writes the whole card; Y2 hands its two lines to the coordinator in its report and
the handoff, and the coordinator folds them into the mayor's four).

**V1.7 (the coordinator).** The lived-in v59 town opened through migration if Y1 bumped the save,
kept as `lived-in-v60.json`; the card checked whole; `docs/handoff.md`'s cold-start section and
`CLAUDE.md` told that 1.7 is the last; decision 390; the release PR `release-1.7` → `main`.
