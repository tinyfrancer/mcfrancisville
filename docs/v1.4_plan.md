# McFrancisVille: plan for 1.4 and 1.5, more to collect, wear, build and see

**Status:** settled 2026-10-25 (decision 367), the afternoon 1.3 shipped (PR #219). The user has
most of a week of usage left and wants it spent on "a fun game": content she will collect, wear,
decorate with and look at, no personal touches (decision 177 stands; dropped again 2026-10-25),
no refactors. Sessions append "**Session X landed** (…, decision n, PR #n)" here as they merge;
patches to her phone are marked ⬆ and cut at the user's word (or by the coordinator, as 1.3 was).

## What it is for

She collects (75 critters, 12 fossils, 8 dolls, 8 squishies), decorates (119 pieces in 60
sets, three rooms and a yard), dresses up (108 pieces) and plays at night, every day. 1.3 made
the game sound and run right; this plan gives her more of what she does with it, timed to the
winter she is about to play through. Every session is content as rows and art, on the seams the
earlier plans made (a critter is a row; a set is a const and a `SUITES` row; a piece of clothing
is a row and a print; a happening is a row; the sky, the photo and the rooms have their
machinery). Nothing punishes, expires or is lost (decision 11); nothing is more than a fortnight
away (decision 360).

**Models:** every session here draws or is judged by eye, so every session is **Opus**; the
review too.

## The sessions

Each is one context window, a draft PR into `v1.4-dev` merged by the session when green, two at
a time by the files they own. Decisions from **370**, one per session. A session that changes the
save bumps `SAVE_VERSION` only in its last commit after merging `v1.4-dev`, one such PR merging at
a time (the save is v55 now).

### 1.4

**G1. Shiny morphs (L; the save).** Every critter (`CRITTERS`, `CRAWLIES`, the fish) has a
**shiny**: a second palette (a `shiny` swap on its row or in a `SHINIES` table in
`src/sprites/critterLooks.ts`, drawn by palette swap, no new grids), dealt about one catch in
twelve for commons, rarer up the tiers, from the day key and the slot so the same critter in the
same slot is shiny for everyone who looks (decision 102's dealing), with a slow sparkle over it in
the world (a `frames` glow or an effect) so she can see one from across the lawn. Caught, it is
the same item with a `shiny` mark: the bag shows the sparkle on its icon, the Cabinet marks each
critter's shiny found beside its first (`Cabinet.shinies`, save v56), the museum's case shows the
shiny when she has donated one (a second nook or the shiny in place), and a figurine of a shiny
is a shiny figurine. Two shelves in `MILESTONES`: a shiny of every family; a shiny of everything.
Today and the small talk know a shiny ("something glittered by the creek"). The rarity test's
year says how long the shinies take (most of a second year is right). A line in 1.4's notes.

**G2. A winter wardrobe and two winter sets (L; never the save).** She plays November to
February next. **Clothes** (`src/data/outfits.ts`, prints in `OUTFIT_ART`, a row each with a
description, in her colours): a duffel coat, a puffer, a long scarf (a new slot or `outer`
layer, read the wardrobe rules), mittens (gloves), a bobble hat and earmuffs (hats), knitted
tights, snow boots, a fair-isle jumper, a cardigan, flannel pyjamas and a dressing gown for
nights in, a onesie (bat), about fourteen pieces, in the boutique's weekly sets and on Cobweb
Corner's shelves from November. **Furniture**: two sets (`SUITES` rows, art in a new
`src/sprites/setsThree.ts`), **Cosy Cabin** (a log stove with a fire that `frames`, a knit rug, a
bearskin-not-a-bear rug, a rocking chair that seats her, a quilt bed, a mug shelf, fairy lights)
and **Midnight Observatory** (a brass telescope that `plays` a tune, a star chart, a globe of the
moon, a velvet chaise, a candle cluster, a window wallpaper of stars), at Gourdon's book and
Cobweb Corner. Every piece drawn to `docs/art_style.md`, looked at on her at Close, both
orientations. A line in 1.4's notes.

**G3. Ice fishing (M; the save if needed).** The pond and the lake are ice in winter and the
creek all year on her skates (`MapZone.isIce`, `FROZEN`). On an ice tile she can walk up to, a
tap casts into a hole (the rod's cast on ice: `Fishing` reads the tile as water if it's ice,
with a drilled hole drawn under the float, `src/render/fishing.ts`); **four winter fish**
(`CRITTERS` rows, `season` the winter months, `where` the pond and the lake, one rare, one
legendary on the full moon, art at 16 and 24 as F3 drew the fish, with shadows under the ice
drawn dim), never more than a fortnight away (decision 360 holds); **an ice hut** as a fifth
town project (`PROJECTS` row, on the pond's edge, three stages, warm inside: a tiny room,
`INTERIORS.iceHut`, with a stove and a hole in the floor she fishes through on any winter day).
The Cabinet and the museum take the four. A line in 1.4's notes. ⬆ **1.4** when G1–G3 are in.

### 1.5

**G4. The night sky (L; the save).** A telescope at the castle lookout (a prop, `telescope`,
beside the gate; or the observatory set's piece, which also works from home at night). On a
clear night (not rain, not fog; `?weather=clear`), walking up opens `src/hud/SkySheet.ts` (a
`SkyApi`): the sky as a field of stars drawn from `src/data/constellations.ts` (twelve, one a
month, spooky-cute shapes: the Bat, the Cauldron, the Cat, the Candle…; and three for holidays),
the month's one findable: she traces it by tapping its stars in order (a game of taps as the
fair's, `score` by the order), and it joins her **star chart** (`Cabinet.stars`, save v57; a
Sky tab in the Cabinet with the chart filling in), a keepsake for each; the meteor shower rare
event (R3) shows every constellation at once for the night. The world's sky (`src/render/sky.ts`)
shows the month's constellation faintly over the castle hill at night once found. Two shelves.
A line in 1.5's notes.

**G5. A photo mode (M; never the save).** A camera in the ☰ tray (and on the quick bar when she
holds nothing): the world freezes to a viewfinder (`?loop=manual`'s stepping, the HUD hidden but
for the shutter, a frame she can drag), a filter (plain, sepia, spooky green, moonlit), her
critters and dolls as stickers from her bag, and a caption; the shutter crops the canvas
(`render/photo.ts`'s `photoOf`, `printOf`) and keeps it through the share sheet
(`hud/keepPhoto.ts`), with a polaroid frame drawn on. Neighbours within three tiles turn to the
camera and pose (a `pose` stance, `neighbourLife`'s wave or a new `smile`), pets sit. A line in
1.5's notes.

**G6. An attic (S; the save).** A third room up a ladder in the back room's wall (a `ROOMS` row,
`attic`, reached as the back room is, `Crossing.room`), a recipe at the workbench (`{ room }`,
wood and candles), a sloped ceiling drawn as a `roomShell` variant with a round window showing
the sky (`windowSky`), its own first size and one extension. A line in 1.5's notes.

**G7. A seventh pet (S; the save if a row needs it).** A possum, Pickle: a `PETS` row, art at the
pets' size in `src/sprites/pets.ts` (walk, sit front and behind, the rests, two tricks: playing
dead and hanging by the tail), accessories (a tiny scarf, a lantern), a job on walks (finds a
snack), lines for pats, at Rufus's shop as the others are sold. A line in 1.5's notes.

**V1.5. Review and 1.5 (S).** The 1.3 lived-in town opened as her phone will through the saves
(v55 → the newest), a few days played by taps and kept as a fixture, perf against 1.3's table,
the notes rows (1.4's as shipped if cut, 1.5's new; folded to one if 1.4 wasn't cut), "Where it
hurts", the art notes, the handoff's cold-start section, `CLAUDE.md`, the release PR.
⬆ **1.5**.
